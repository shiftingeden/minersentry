What it does

MinerSentry watches your home or small-scale ASIC mining fleet over your local Wi-Fi — no cloud account, no middleman. It supports Bitmain Antminer (S21, L7 and more) on stock, Vnish, Braiins OS and LuxOS firmware, plus Canaan Avalon Nano 3 / 3S.

  • Live hashrate, temperature, fan speed, power and best share
  • Miners grouped by type and algorithm (SHA-256, Scrypt, Equihash)
  • Per-coin solo block-odds and best-share tracking
  • Alerts for low hashrate, overheating and offline miners
  • Switch active pools and reboot miners individually or by group
  • Lock Screen / Dynamic Island Live Activity and a Home Screen widget
  • Private iCloud sync of your miners and settings

Getting started

  • Add a miner: tap +, then either run a Wi-Fi scan or enter the miner's IP address directly.
  • Nothing found when scanning? Make sure your iPhone is on the same Wi-Fi network as your miners, and that Local Network access is enabled in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Local Network ▸ MinerSentry.
  • Controls: open a miner to see details, switch its pool, or reboot it; the Controls tab acts on the whole fleet.
