Privacy Policy

MinerSentry · Last updated June 19, 2026

Summary

MinerSentry does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. The app runs entirely on your device and your own private iCloud.

What the app accesses

  • Your local network. MinerSentry connects to the mining hardware on your own Wi-Fi network to read status (hashrate, temperature, power, shares) and to send control commands you initiate (switching pools, rebooting). This communication stays on your local network and never reaches us.
  • A public block-difficulty lookup. If you enable solo block-odds, the app requests current network difficulty from a public blockchain data API. These requests contain no personal information and are not linked to you.

Where your data lives

  • Your list of miners and app settings are stored on your device.
  • If you use iCloud, those same settings sync through your own private iCloud account (Apple's key-value store). We have no access to this data.
  • Miner credentials you enter are stored on your device solely to authenticate with your own hardware and are never transmitted to us or any third party.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data, because the app does not collect any. No data is sent to MinerSentry's developer or to advertisers.

Children's privacy

MinerSentry is a utility app that collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
